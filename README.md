# Jesse Ray McMillen

**Open-source superior energy production systems**

I design and publish fully open, single-person-scale thermal energy systems that capture, store, and convert more useful energy per square metre than conventional photovoltaics — while remaining passive, modular, and buildable with common materials.

## The Core Work: Dual-Battery Thermal Architecture

Hot air is drawn through a desiccant, then through a subterranean volumetric open-cell aerocement labyrinth with massive internal surface area. Cold is extracted into a water tank. The air is then heated (by solar aero-disc or rocket mass heater) and the heat is extracted into a second water tank. Air exits near ambient temperature.

The temperature difference between the two tanks drives mechanical work and electricity. The entire primary cycle is passive.

### Two Implementations
- **Solar Aero-Disc** — fully passive, sun-driven
- **Rocket Mass Heater + Black Locust** — carbon-sequestering fuel, works day or night

### Why This Beats Photovoltaics
- Captures 4–5× more total energy (heat + cold) per m² than PV produces as electricity
- Simultaneously produces heat and cold
- Delivers energy in the forms people actually use
- Extracts mechanical work directly from ΔT
- Stores energy cheaply in water
- Can run carbon-negative
- Almost zero external energy input for the primary cycle
- Fully modular and single-person buildable
