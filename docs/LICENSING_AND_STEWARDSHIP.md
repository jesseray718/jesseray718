# Licensing and cooperation

The maintainer-approved baseline is GNU GPL version 3 for software,
scripts and firmware, and CC BY-SA 4.0 for hardware design documentation,
manuals, diagrams and other non-software material. This is a category-based
split, not a blanket choice of either license for every file.

Existing copyright notices, third-party terms and per-file SPDX/version
options remain in force. Do not normalize GPL-3.0-only and
GPL-3.0-or-later into one label. Contributors retain their copyrights;
contributions must be offered under the applicable category's terms with
appropriate permission. No copyright assignment or proprietary relicensing
permission is requested by this policy.

## Scope and limits

Commercial cooperation and participation are welcome subject to the
applicable license conditions. Copyleft is not a prohibition on businesses.
GPLv3 does not generally require publication merely for running a modified
program as a service. This repair does not adopt AGPL.

CC BY-SA licenses copyright, not patent or trademark rights. Applying it
to documentation is not a guarantee of reciprocal licensing of every
manufactured implementation. CERN-OHL-S-2.0 remains an assessment option
for future hardware design releases; it is not adopted here.

No license text is presented as a guarantee against organizational capture.
Existing releases retain the permissions already granted. The historical
two-line AGPL root notice is preserved in Git history. This clarification
asserts no power to relicense third-party or independently contributed work.
Specific notices take precedence; unresolved conflicts require rights review.

## Stewardship direction

Keep source and releases independently mirrorable. Record material licensing,
asset-transfer and governance proposals publicly. Seek independent review
before changing these boundaries. Do not concentrate control of domains,
release keys, funds or project identity in one external company. Credit
contributors and document maintainer succession.

These are stewardship commitments, not a claim that a legal entity,
multi-party governance, trademark registration or GitHub protection rules
have already been established. No branch protection is changed by this repair.
